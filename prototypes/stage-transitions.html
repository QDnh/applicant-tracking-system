Hiring stage rules

Question this answers: do the stage-movement rules from our design interview feel right once you actually push applications through them, including misclicks, repeated clicking, illegal moves and reapplying?

  • Active stages: Applied → Screening → Interview → Final Review → Offer. Final stages: Hired, Rejected, Withdrawn. Nothing moves out of a final stage.
  • From an active stage you can go one step forward, one step back, or to Reject or Withdraw. Hire is only possible from Offer.
  • Hire, Reject and Withdraw ask "are you sure?" first (the real app will use hx-confirm).
  • Every move adds one row to the stage history. Nothing is ever collapsed or deleted.
  • A candidate can have only one active application per job. Once it reaches a final stage, they can apply again.
